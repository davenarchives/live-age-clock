Meet the revamped experience

Built for live, glanceable age tracking

Enter your birth details once and jump into a full-screen clock that updates every second. Swap between our Date Timer, Decimal stream, or the playful Digital Flip board whenever you want.

Date Timer Decimal View Digital Flip

How it works

  1. Choose your month, day, year, and (optionally) your time of birth.
  2. Hit Start Clock to hide the form and launch the live dashboard.
  3. Use the Themes dropdown in the header to toggle between the three visual modes.
  4. The Date Timer highlights years through seconds, Decimal shows a rolling fractional year, and Digital Flip animates each tick.
  5. Select Reset in the bottom-left to return to the entry form.

Why the new interface?

The refreshed UI keeps the layout bold, centered, and legible on any screen. The turquoise navigation bar matches the live experience, while the hero controls and clock views share typography and color tokens for consistency.

  • Single-focus screens — inputs and clocks never compete for space.
  • Theme-aware design — every mode inherits the same palette for seamless switching.
  • Keyboard-friendly inputs — dropdowns, number fields, and time pickers respond to shortcuts.

What’s next

We’re exploring custom palettes, saved birthdays, and additional clock styles. Drop ideas in the GitHub repo or fork it to build your own twist.
